Vendor Quick Notes

Workflow Gate

  • Check vendor docs before assuming modem, board, or AT behavior.
  • Validate a new AT sequence from terminal or serial first.
  • Expose it in firmware only after response, timing, and failure behavior are clear.
  • Then test the firmware action here over MQTT.
